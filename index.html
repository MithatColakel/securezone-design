How to review

  1. Pick a page. At the top: Screens (every screen of the app) and Paywalls (every paywall, offer and Adapty screen, per placement and audience), each for iPhone, iPad and Mac, and the Design system.
  2. Find a frame. Use the list on the left, or move around the canvas. Each row on the canvas is one screen or one placement with its states: English first, then Arabic.
  3. Look closely. Double-click a frame to open it at full size. Use ← and → to go to the next frame.
  4. Comment. Press C (or Comment) and click the spot you mean. Write your note and save. A numbered pin marks the spot.
  5. Send. Open Comments on the left, press Copy all and paste the text into the task or the card. The designer gets the frame names and the exact spots.

Comments stay only in this browser until you copy them. Frame links: #f=<frame> selects a frame, #open=<frame> opens it, #p=<page> opens a page, #ds=<card> opens a Design System card.
